*Every asset listed below **exists in this repository** and was inspected during the portfolio
pass. Nothing here is fabricated, and no stock or generic image is substituted for a project
visual. §3 lists what does not exist and must be captured manually.*

---

## 1 · What exists

**Two shaded 3-D renders**, generated in-repository by `scripts/render_assembly.py` from the
released STL exports composed with the occurrence transforms in the assembly run record. They
are **derived from repository evidence, not drawn**: the script reconciles every occurrence's
transformed mesh bounding box against the bounding box Fusion actually observed, prints the
deviation, and `--strict` refuses to render past tolerance. Reproduce with:

```bash
python scripts/render_assembly.py --strict
```

**Reconciliation, as measured:** 18 of 19 occurrences agree to **≤ 0.072 mm** — inward chord
error from tessellation. `SEWCP-902_SADDLE` is tessellated at 340 triangles and deviates up to
**3.864 mm** on two faces of its bounding box, entirely inward, while its mesh volume agrees with
the observed solid to **0.018 %**. Applying `z_axis_scale = −1.0` to the three inverted alignment
pins is the difference between **0.0000 mm** and **9.5000 mm** on those three — the record carries
that field, and the render reads it.

Also **14 generated drawing sheets** — SVG and PDF, produced deterministically by
`drawings/generate.py`, every dimension carrying a provenance entry. These are the released
engineering record; the renders are presentation.

### Notes on use

- **The renders are presentation; the drawings are the record.** A number read off a render has
  been read off a tessellation. Cite the drawing or the run record for any dimension.
- **SVG over PDF for the web.** GitHub renders repository SVGs inline; the PDFs are the release
  record. Both are byte-identical to what `drawings/generate.py` produces.
- The drawing sheets are **white-background line drawings**. They read correctly in a light theme
  and remain legible in a dark one.
- **Do not crop a title block off** a drawing used as a portfolio visual. The title block carries
  the revision and date, and a drawing without it looks like an illustration rather than a
  released document.
- The renders' caption bar already carries *"Nothing physical has been built."* **Keep it.** A
  shaded render is the single most likely artifact in this repository to be mistaken for a
  photograph of hardware.
