## Notes

**Why the extension fields are lawful without a schema change.** `SCH-task.schema.json`
declares `"additionalProperties": true`. The eight `required` fields are all present above;
`status`, `depends_on`, `consumes`, `produces`, `blocked_by`, `read_scope`, `write_scope`,
`qa`, `context_budget` and `checkpoint` are additional properties the schema admits. No
schema is edited and no manifest field changes.

**Role.** `software.software-engineer` is enabled in `BINDING.enabled_agents` by
`AIEF-AMD-011` and assigned in `ROSTER.md` at rank-1 direction. The verifier is
`qa-engineer`, a different role, as `LAW-05` requires.
