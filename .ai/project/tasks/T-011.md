## Why this task exists

`DEC-21` ruled *preserve*. An independent review measured its factual basis and found it wrong
in both halves — the count, and the twin claims that the name was already in the commit history
and that the repository was already public. The owner reopened it, chose sanitization, and set
the boundary: everything up to the publication gate, nothing past it.

The task is deliberately narrow. It removes one identifier. It does not clean up the repository,
does not repair `ENGINEERING.md` §7's contradictory publication state, does not touch the open
`X-08`/`X-09`/`X-10` failures, and does not certify itself.
