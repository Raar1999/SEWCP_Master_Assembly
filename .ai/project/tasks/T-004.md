## Notes

**This is the QA handoff the architecture exists to make cheap.** The verifier reads this
record, T-001's acceptance criteria, and R-001's conclusion and pinned digests. It does not
read the author's session, and it does not rediscover scope. Independence is preserved
because the criteria are binary and the digests are recomputed, not because the verifier
trusts the account.

Blocked until T-001 is COMPLETE and R-001 is published; `graph.build_plan` derives that,
it is not recorded by hand.
