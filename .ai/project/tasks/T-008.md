## Notes

**LAW-05 is why this task exists as a separate record.** `T-007` authored every disposition,
every specification edit and all three standing checks. It cannot verify them. The write scope
above is deliberately **one file**: a verifier that can edit the artifact under audit is not a
verifier.

The audit was directed to **attack** the checks rather than run them — restore the superseded
clocking and require `aief_clearance` to fail; edit a frozen artifact without an approval and
require the chain to collapse; blank a disposition and require the gate criterion to flip. A
check that has only ever been observed passing is indistinguishable from a check that cannot
fail, which is the defect class `VER-014` recorded four times.
