## Notes

**Two findings this task did not inherit.** Both were found by tightening a rule rather than by
reading a record, which is the pattern `R-015` §4 recorded and the reason the rules were tightened.

**1 · `C6` had been passing `ECR-D-001` on a report that says it is not closed.** `VER-014`
declares `status: ECR-D-001 NOT CLOSED`. The predicate scanned for `FAIL`, `NOT CLEARED` and
`NOT VERIFIED`; that string contains none of them, so `C6` reported `ECR-D-001` satisfied while
`VER-014` §6 read *"`ECR-D-001` is NOT CLOSED after four rounds. `LC-M04-EXIT` `C6` is not
satisfied for it"* and `OPEN_ITEMS_REGISTER.md` row `OI-V-11` said the same. **The index was more
truthful than the instrument.** `VER-016` F-12 had inspected this exact predicate and called its
direction *"fail-safe"*. It was fail-safe only for the wording `VER-016` happened to try.

**2 · The reports were being read on a line ending.** `VER-001` … `VER-014` are CRLF files and
`VER-015`/`VER-016` are LF. An intermediate form of `_load_reports` parsed the raw decode rather
than the DC-1-normalised text, and the YAML fence pattern requires a bare `\n` — so every CRLF
report parsed to an empty mapping and `C6` reported *"no verification report declares it as
subject"* for `ECR-D-001`, discarding four cold rounds. Caught by the gate output disagreeing
with the previous run, fixed at the normalisation, and held by
`test_verification_chain.py::test_crlf_reports_parse`.

**Scope held.** No `spec/**` artifact was touched — the frozen set is byte-identical and its
aggregate is unchanged. No Stage 6 work, no `MANIFEST.lock`, no ledger entry, no tag, no push.

**Recorded, not repaired.** `VER-016` F-08 (`APR-001`…`APR-013` carry no `ecr:` field) is a
convention boundary, not a defect in those approvals: the field enters at `APR-016`. Adding it
would mean rewriting thirteen human-approval records post hoc to carry metadata their approver
never wrote, and this task will not do that silently. Recorded as `OI-C-13`. `VER-016` F-14
(`CONTEXT_TIERS.md` caps) is under `.ai/core/**` and awaits the Stage 1 re-emission `OQ-14`
withholds.
