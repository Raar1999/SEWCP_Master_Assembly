## Notes

**This task is recorded, not executed.** It is BLOCKED on three independent grounds, each
derived rather than asserted: `depends_on` T-002 and T-003 are not COMPLETE; `consumes`
R-002 and R-003 do not exist; and `blocked_by` OI-C-09 is open in `OPEN_ITEMS.md`.

**`blocked_by` was `[OQ-14]` until `S-2026-08-12-01`**, when `OQ-14` was discharged — the owner
authorised Stage 6 at `S-2026-08-11-06` and the canonical emission was then performed. It is
re-pointed at `OI-C-09`, which this record's own `forbidden_actions` already names and which
remains open; the ground is unchanged in kind, only in identity.

**Stage 6 was executed outside this task record**, directly under the owner's rank-1
instruction, and this task was not the vehicle. It is therefore **not** marked COMPLETE and
must not be: `AC-1` (two archives agreeing on DC-5) is unevidenced here, and the execution
preceded the `OI-C-09` delivery this record forbids executing without. `AC-2` — *"boot step
B2a executes and passes against the emitted lock"* — **is** satisfied, verified at the
`S-2026-08-12-01` boot. Recording that one criterion met does not discharge the task, and the
divergence between what this record required and how the emission actually happened is stated
here rather than resolved by editing the record to match the outcome.

`write_scope` names `.ai/core/MANIFEST.lock`, which lies inside the framework-protected set.
That is lawful **only** for Compiler Stage 6, which is the emitting authority for layer L7 —
and it is exactly why check X-04 flags this task's write scope for explicit review rather
than allowing it silently. Recording the task does not authorize the write; OQ-14 does.
