## DEC-01 — ECR-D-013: RF strap terminal holes vs plate tap clocking

**Question.** `spec/08` RS-D07 (2× Ø6.6 at 25.0 centres) cannot close
against `spec/01` CP-IF-8 (taps at r = 137, 98.7°/111.3° → 29.94 mm
in-plane centre distance). Which side moves?

**Governing sources.** spec/01 CP-IF-8; spec/08 RS-D06/D07, §2.2, §9;
params `rf_tap_half_ang`, `rf_land_*`; ECR-Q-010 (same land already
dimensioned two ways); clearance framework (`aief_clearance`).

**Observed facts.** Both faces are planar (land = CP bottom face,
Z = 20); tap positions project into the pad plane at (136.176, ±14.971) mm
in the strap's radial frame (x = radius along the 105° ray). The pad is
50 × 18 (RS-D06) — holes at ±14.971 leave 10.03 mm edge distance
(≥ 1.5 d = 9.9 for Ø6.6). The plate is the verified, most expensive part;
its taps clear the channel keep-out, the RTD and choke patterns, and were
independently clearance-checked. The strap is the compliant, cheapest,
explicitly adaptable element.

**Alternatives.**
- (a) Re-pitch the strap holes to coincide with the taps — one part
  re-drilled in CAD; edge distance verified; electrically neutral-to-better
  (wider current spread on the 60 mm land).
- (b) Re-clock the plate taps to a 25.0 chord (99.77°/110.23°) — modifies
  the governing plate, re-opens its clearance evidence and the re-homed
  lineage, for no functional gain. **Rejected.**
- (c) Slot the strap holes — degrades positional determinacy the bracket
  exists to provide (RS-D07 ⌖ Ø0.5 Ⓜ). **Rejected.**

**Selected.** (a). `spec/08` RS-D07 now defines the holes as coincident
with the CP-IF-8 taps (29.94 mm centres in the pad plane), the strap model
re-drilled at (136.176, ±14.971).

**Physical/manufacturing reasoning.** Ø6.6 over M6 keeps ±0.3 assembly
float; silver-plated joint area unchanged; drilling before forming
unchanged; flat-pattern hole stations move outboard 2.47 mm and split
±14.971 — all inside the blank.

**Affected.** spec/08 (RS-D07); SEWCP-901 model + drawing + flat pattern;
`rs_hole_pitch` parameter replaced by tap-projected coordinates; assembly
unaffected (placement unchanged). Gate: C7 clears once dispositioned and
applied; C5 re-registration under APR-029/APR-030.

## DEC-02 — ECR-Q-012: SEWCP-902 support architecture

**Question.** The base-seated saddle contradicts RF-IF-3 ("mounts to the
RF-hot plate, not to ground") and SB-D04 (≥ 8.0 to Base Plate), and a
grounded contact would short the strap.

**Governing sources.** spec/08 RF-IF-3, SB-D01..D04, RS-D04, §2.2/§2.3
(h = 8 electrical gap), §12; spec/01 CP-IF-8 (land, bottom face);
observed strap installed-form geometry.

**Observed facts.** Strap run mid-plane 8.0 (top 8.25, underside 7.75),
run spans radial 35–77.7 at azimuth 105°; S rises 77.7→106; pad flat
106→146 at Z 19.5–20. CP bottom face Z = 20 is RF-hot. Any support
material below Z = 8.0 violates the analyzed Paschen gap to ground.

**Alternatives.**
- (a) Plate-hung hanger, support from **above**: U-plate (5.0 web) bolted
  to the CP bottom face at r = 137, 105° ± 16.73° (± 40 mm arc per
  RF-IF-3); rails outside the strap width (±39.4 from the strap axis);
  saddle drop at radial 68–76 bearing on the strap **top** face at
  Z = 8.25 (sets RS-D04 mid = 8.0 exactly); lateral cheeks to Z = 8.0
  (the spec minimum). All bracket material ≥ 8.0 from ground; bracket is
  RF-hot with the strap — no short by construction. **Selected.**
- (b) Base-seated dielectric saddle — new material qualification in
  vacuum/RF (spec/08 §2.4 warns against dielectric bridging: DR-12
  forbids solid insulators bridging conductor to ground — a ceramic
  saddle IS such a bridge). **Rejected on DR-12.**
- (c) Support from below at h < 8 — violates the Paschen clearance.
  **Rejected.**

**Selected geometry (dimensioned).** One-piece 6061 machining: U-plate
web 5.0 (Z 15→20), plan: rails 10 wide at ±(34.44…44.44) from strap axis,
radial 68→140.2; feet 18 × 18 at the tap stations (137, ±16.73°) with
2× Ø6.6; crossbar full-width at radial 68–76; saddle drop block
(radial 68–76, ±31) Z 8.25→15; cheeks (radial 68–76, ±(25.25…30.25))
Z 8.0→8.25, guiding the 50.0 strap with 0.5 total lateral clearance.
Bearing face 8.25 = strap top; strap preloaded up by its own S-compliance
(RS-D05's purpose). Verified clear of: strap S (drop ends 1.7 before the
rise), pad flat (rails pass at ±34+ where the pad is ±25), VP flange, RTD
ports/retainers, locators, land taps, channel envelope (feet at r = 137 >
125). M6 × 16 SHCS, 6.0 N·m: grip 5.0, engagement 11 in M6 × 12 taps.

**Affected.** spec/08 §5.2 (SB rows re-dimensioned); spec/01 CP-IF-8
(+2 bracket taps at r = 137, 88.27°/121.73°, M6 × 12 — bottom face, clear
of every keep-out); SEWCP-902 full remodel; SEWCP-200 +2 taps; assembly
saddle occurrence re-placed (rotate 105°, translate Z = 20, band
[8.0, 20.0]); drawings 200/901/902/000; BOM (dims note).

### DEC-02 addendum — bracket tap placement corrected by observed evidence

The first implementation placed the taps per RF-IF-3's literal "Ø274 BC
± 40 mm" → (137, 88.27°/121.73°). The build's ACC-VOL check failed
(504.8 mm³ removed vs 678.6 expected): the taps intersect the 90°/120°
outer choke slots (through-slots at Ø270 BC, 5.5 × 7.0). Feasibility
sweep at r = 137: the slot at 90° and the land edge at 93° close the
lower window; 117°–120° closes the upper one — **no compliant position
exists inside ±40 mm**. RF-IF-3's window is itself infeasible; nothing at
Ø274 BC was ever clearance-checked for bracket taps. Corrected placement
**r = 150, 88°/122°**, verified against: choke slots (15.8 mm centre
distance vs 8.4 needed), ring bolts at 112.5°/67.5° (24.9 mm), locator
slots (74 mm), land outer radius 146 (tap inner edge 147), channel
envelope (125), RTD ports, coolant stubs, plate OD (foot edge 159 < 160),
and the top-face ring taps (laterally separated, no z-overlap conflict).
The failed attempt's revert disposition restored the verified plate
automatically (RUN-20260811T224345, failure_disposition_revert).
Both spec rows re-issued under APR-031/APR-032.

## DEC-03 — ECR-Q-011: FSW minimum rib

**Question.** Is the 5.0 mm design rib manufacturable for the internal
FSW rib passes, or must the channel re-route at a larger pitch?

**Governing sources.** spec/01 §6 steps 4–6, CP-D05..D08, FMEA #1;
MDR-001 §4/§5; ECR-Q-011 record; published FSW practice for aluminum
lap/lid closure welds.

**Reasoning.** The FSW shoulder rides the **continuous 6.00 lid**
(CP-D08) — shoulder engagement does not consume rib width; only the
**probe tip** must land on rib material. For 6.0–7.0 penetration a tapered
probe (root Ø5–6 at the lid top, tip Ø3.5–4.0) is standard practice; a
5.0 rib carries a Ø4.0 tip with ±0.5 lateral margin, adequate under seam
tracking ≤ ±0.5 (standard on production FSW gantries). Widening the rib
to 6.0 would force a full re-route (pitch 16 → 17+), shorten the developed
path a further ~6%, and discard verified geometry for no demonstrated
need. The residual (a specific supplier's tooling) is an ordinary RFQ
capability confirmation, not an open design input.

**Selected.** Rib **5.0 confirmed**; a tool-envelope constraint is added
to `spec/01` §6 step 5: tapered probe, tip Ø ≤ 4.0, seam tracking
≤ ±0.5 mm, penetration 6.5–7.0 (0.5–1.0 into the rib). Channel unchanged;
no re-route. RFQ folders carry the constraint (drawing note already
present).

## DEC-04 — Coolant developed path: 1.64 m as-routed vs ≈ 2.2 m basis

**Question.** What does the ≈ 2.2 m figure bind, and is the as-routed
1.64 m acceptable?

**Classification.** `spec/01` §2.1 lists "Developed path length ≈ 2.2 m —
Serpentine layout" in the **design-basis table** (an estimate feeding the
wetted-area line). It is not a numbered requirement; the binding items
are CP-02 (4.0 L/min at ΔP < 1.5 bar — flow bench), CP-05 (proof), CP-11
(uniformity — thermal map). MDR-001 §4 **proves** ≈ 2.2 m was never
simultaneously achievable with the frozen keep-outs (outboard
constant-radius band empty; feasible band 25 mm wide).
**Classification: design-basis estimate, not a hard requirement.**

**Margin evaluation at 1.64 m.** Coolant ΔT = Q/(ṁ·c_p) = 10.8 K —
path-independent, unchanged. h ≈ 6 500 W/m²K — section-driven, unchanged.
Wetted area scales to ≈ 0.060 m² → film ΔT at 3 kW rises ≈ 5.8 → ≈ 7.7 K:
absorbed by the plate's isothermalizing thickness and bounded by CP-11's
thermal-map verification, which remains a release condition. ΔP **falls**
roughly with length (−25%), *increasing* CP-02 margin against the
< 1.5 bar limit that ECR-D-002 flagged as the at-risk figure.

**Selected.** Accept the as-routed 1.64 m; record this derivation;
CP-02 and CP-11 remain explicitly physical/analysis verification —
**CAD COMPLETE — PHYSICAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. No spec change (the
§2.1 "≈" table is a basis record; the routing authority MDR-001 already
documents the geometry that governs).
