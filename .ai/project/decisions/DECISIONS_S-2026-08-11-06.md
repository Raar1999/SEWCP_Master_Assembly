## DEC-05 — ECR-D-006: the framework manifest registry divergence

**Question.** `framework/framework.manifest.json` does not reproduce against
its registered digest, and `aief_approval verify` reports the working tree at
`920eb6ee…` with **no approval binding that state**, voiding six approvals.

**Governing sources.** LAW-01 (clauses 1, 5), LAW-02 (clauses 2, 4, 5),
LAW-10 (clauses 1, 2); `AIEF-AMD-008` §AMD-21; `AIEF-AMD-014` §AMD-53;
`ECR-D-005` / `APR-001` as precedent; `GATES.md`'s exclusion of this path.

**What the audit found that the ECR record did not carry.** The ECR names one
defect. There are **two**. `AIEF-AMD-014`'s eleven manifest changes were
**applied** and **every record of their authority was omitted**: `APR-014` and
`APR-015` were never filed, the registry row was never re-registered, and
`AIEF-AMD-014` itself was never added to the registry under the AMD-21
criterion it declares in its own § *Blast Radius*. `check_v24` names the
omission in terms. Nobody had noticed for three sessions.

**Evidence.** The attribution rests on a git object, not a reconstruction.
`git show 8546960:framework/framework.manifest.json` reproduces to
`ae16ccac…9d8395aa` — `APR-006`'s `subject_hash`, recorded four sessions
before the defect. The delta from it to the current artifact was taken
structurally over the parsed JSON, with `files[]` and `dependencies.edges`
compared as sets: **27 changed leaves, zero removals, every one carrying its
own authorising citation inside the value.** The counts match each
instrument's own enumeration exactly — AMD-012's three, `APR-012`'s thirteen,
and `AIEF-AMD-014`'s eleven partitioning 4 new members + 2 edges + 1 cap + 4
text extensions.

**Alternatives.** B revert (discards three approved amendments and every
Stage 6 construction — rejected); C waive (Stage 6 would seal an unproven
chain into `build_provenance.source_manifest_dc1` — rejected); D register the
unreproducible value (a hash is never invented — rejected); E repair the
manifest row only (`V-24` still fails on the AMD-21 criterion — rejected).

**Selected. A — re-register at the measured digest and re-affirm the change
sets.** Manifest re-registered at `920eb6ee…37814090` (`APR-033`);
`AIEF-AMD-014` registered at `07ced758…be639945` (`APR-034`); registry 29 → 30;
DC-2 recomputed to `19989657…8ddc07e`; `STATE.frozen_set_hash` follows.

**One departure from `AIEF-AMD-014` §AMD-53's recommendation, on evidentiary

## DEC-06 — ECR-D-014: the lock token cap is unsatisfiable by any conforming lock

**Question.** The first authorised Stage 6 build halted:
`core/MANIFEST.lock: governing 6469 > cap 200`. Which octets does
`files[manifest-lock].token_cap` bound?

**Governing sources.** `budget_measurement_record.lock_self_measurement`
(`AIEF-AMD-013` §AMD-45); `core_aggregate.lock_serialisation`
(`AIEF-AMD-010` §AMD-27); `sch-core-manifest`; MI-4 and `AIEF-FRZ-001` §1.8;
`AIEF-AMD-013` §AMD-41 §5 (the determinately-inadmissible finding);
`AIEF-AMD-014` §§AMD-50/51.

**The conflict.** AMD-45 measures the whole serialised document. AMD-27 says
*"aggregate_digest precedes files **so the T1 digest read stays within the
200-token cap**"* — a statement that the cap bounds a **prefix**, and the only
reading under which member ordering can bear on the count at all. Under AMD-45's
reading, AMD-27's clause states a purpose it cannot serve. Both are in the same
frozen artifact.

**Measured, dual-family, not marginal.** Whole lock 6469 TF-2 / 5334 TF-1
against 200 — 32×. `files` alone 5198 (75 path-digest pairs, schema-required,
content normatively fixed). **Schema-required members with `files` removed
still measure 285.** No conforming lock of any content meets 200.

**Alternatives.** Raise to 6469 — MI-4 would go 5904 → 12173, destroying the
§1.8 derivation (rejected, on §AMD-41 §5(iii)). Raise to 296, the only value
MI-4 admits — fits by five tokens, consumes **all 96** remaining headroom, and
leaves the cap bounding a region containing `build_id`, whose length no clause
bounds; measured 280→319 across identifier lengths (rejected). Null cap —
deliberately creates the `OI-C-08` exposure and discards AMD-51 (rejected).
Shrink the covered set — trades integrity coverage for a token count
(**rejected outright**). Halt and reserve to the owner, per `AIEF-AMD-013`
§AMD-41 — considered seriously; §AMD-41 reserved because its remedy space held
live discretionary options, and this one does not, and the owner's instruction
directs repair at the owning layer.

**Selected. A — scope the measured quantity to the boot-read prefix
(§AMD-54), and move `aggregate_digest` to second position (§AMD-55).** Prefix
measures **69 TF-2 / 56 TF-1** against 200 — a 2.9× margin, and it contains no
run-scoped octet, so the cap bounds a quantity the specification determines.
**MI-4 is 5904 of 6000 before and after; no `token_cap` changes.**

**Why the reorder is not cosmetic.** With §AMD-54 alone the prefix carries
`build_provenance` and measures 291 — still a breach, and varying with the
length of a free-form build identifier. A check that depends on what someone
types is not a check.

**Free to make, and only now.** No `core/MANIFEST.lock` had ever been emitted,
so no DC-5 release digest, no `core_digest_pin` and no archive existed to
invalidate. The same reorder after the first canonical build would have been
expensive.

**Residual, recorded not closed.** The whole-document size of the lock is now
bounded by nothing — `OI-C-14`.

## DEC-07 — Correction of record: `DECISIONS_S-2026-08-11-05` DEC-04's CP-02 margin claim

**Not an edit to the historical decision record.** DEC-04 stands as written;
this is the correction, attributed and dated, in the place corrections belong.

**What DEC-04 says.** *"ΔP **falls** roughly with length (−25 %),
*increasing* CP-02 margin against the < 1.5 bar limit that ECR-D-002 flagged as
the at-risk figure."*

**What is true.** The −25 % is correct **for the length term in isolation**.
Read as a statement about `CP-02` margin it is **the wrong sign**, because it
omits the section term `ECR-D-002` introduced. Darcy–Weisbach with a Blasius
friction factor, taken as a **ratio** so that fluid properties and the model
constant cancel and no temperature assumption enters:

| Term | Ratio | |
|---|---|---|
| Section, depth 8.0 → 6.0 | 2.046 | **+105 %** |
| Length, 2.20 → 1.64 m | 0.745 | −25 % |
| **Net against the sizing basis** | **1.525** | **+53 %** |

**`CP-02` margin decreased by roughly half again. It did not increase.**

`spec/01` — which governs — always said the correct thing: *"ΔP rises
materially … The hydraulic direction is adverse and is open. `CP-02` shall be
verified before build release."*

**DEC-04's conclusion is unaffected and was right**: accept the as-routed
1.64 m, assert no ΔP value, keep `CP-02` as physical verification. Only its
stated reason was wrong. **No geometry, dimension or disposition changes.**
Consequence: `CP-02` is the requirement most at risk in the specification and
should be the **first** bench test on the first FSW-closed article. Recorded in
full at [`../verification/PVR-001_Physical_Verification_Record_And_Test_Matrix.md`](../verification/PVR-001_Physical_Verification_Record_And_Test_Matrix.md) §3.3.

## DEC-09 — `V-25` working-tree CRLF: the predicted failure, realised and cured

**Question.** `V-25` — an AMD-31 compile-time precondition of Stage 6 — was
failing on **62 files** carrying CR in the working tree.

**Root cause, already on record.** `OI-V-05` FIND-Q3-4: *"`core.autocrlf=true`
with no `.gitattributes` — a future checkout may rewrite working-tree files to
CRLF and break the literal V-25 working-tree condition; a `.gitattributes`
declaring LF for `.ai/**` is a `repository-engineer` action."* It was recorded
as a prediction. It had happened.

**Selected.** Apply the ruled remedy. `.gitattributes` gains
`text eol=lf` for `.ai/**`, `spec/**`, `framework/**` — the whole `V-25` domain,
every tracked file in which is text — and the 62 working-tree files were
normalised to LF.

**No registered digest moves**, and this was verified rather than assumed: DC-1
normalises CRLF to LF before hashing, and all 62 digests were compared before
and after. Zero moved. A fresh `git checkout` of a previously-CRLF file was
performed and the result contains no CR.
